\item \textbf{Abstract and Section 1: motivation and related work.}
The central motivation and contribution statement are retained; no substantive
rewrite is claimed. \checkauthor{Correct the Point CHR/CQHR distinction and
add shape-template related work.} These changes are necessary for accurate
positioning of the new comparisons.

\item \textbf{Section 2: setup and motivating constructions.}
The supplied revision renames and reorganizes the preliminaries, clarifies

\checkauthor{Review the new working-hypothesis conditional-coverage claim,

protocols and CQR model settings are explicit. The original energy example remains in the body. These changes
answer requests for coordinate detail, broader baselines, and computational
evidence; the Point CHR infinite-volume case is now explained by its allocation.
