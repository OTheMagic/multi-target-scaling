\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Abstract and Section 1: motivation and related work.}
The central motivation and contribution statement are retained; no substantive
rewrite is claimed. \checkauthor{Correct the Point CHR/CQHR distinction and
add shape-template related work.} These changes are necessary for accurate
positioning of the new comparisons.

\item \textbf{Section 2: setup and motivating constructions.}
The supplied revision renames and reorganizes the preliminaries, clarifies
joint exchangeability and simultaneous versus marginal coverage, strengthens
vector/scalar distinctions, discusses ties, and introduces a common
parameterized population-oracle formulation. This makes the statistical
target and the role of standardization more explicit.
The latest edit removes the unqualified shift-invariance claim and replaces

\checkauthor{Reconcile capped-score ties with the implementation and qualify

construction in that notation, and corrects the exceptional empty-cell sign.

pointwise oracle-acceptance implication, improving mathematical precision.
\checkauthor{Review the search-lemma endpoint inequality and complete the
common-event argument in the piecewise coverage proof.}

remains in the body. Sampling protocols and CQR settings are explicit. These changes
answer requests for coordinate detail, broader baselines, and computational
evidence; the Point CHR infinite-volume case is now explained by its allocation.
